# Guide: The Negotiation / Pushback Round

A customer or stakeholder pushes back on scope, timeline, price, or expectations,
and you have to hold the line without losing the relationship.

**What great looks like**
1. **Find the underlying interest** — ask why before answering: what deadline,
   metric, or boss is behind the demand, and what "done" really means to them.
2. **Trade, don't absorb** — make scope, time, and quality explicit levers: "We
   can add X in 4 weeks if Y moves to phase 2."
3. **Say no with a reason and an alternative** — name the risk in their terms
   (the data can't support that promise yet) and offer what you *can* commit to.
4. **Confirm it in writing** — recap what was agreed, what wasn't, owners, and
   dates in a short follow-up so nobody remembers it differently.

**Anti-patterns:** saying yes to keep the peace; arguing positions instead of
interests; vague verbal agreements with no follow-up.
